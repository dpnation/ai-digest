OpenAI says its agents now finish about half of 2-4 hour research tasks

OpenAI's internal data shows agents completing nearly half of tasks that would take a researcher two to four hours, up from about a quarter in March. The median researcher runs about $2.4K of agent work a day, and two-thirds run four or more agents at once.
