Sketch-first prompting plus a short handoff summary

Open a PMM drafting task with: "Don't start yet. I want to [task]. Ask me up to five multiple-choice questions, then sketch the plan and wait for my go-ahead." When the chat gets long, ask for a handoff summary under 150 words and start fresh; compare message count and output quality against your usual approach.
