🛠️ try this

Run a must-keep check on every AI revision

Before asking an AI to change a messaging doc or battlecard, write a short list of what it must keep (claims, proof points, required caveats). After the edit, give it both versions plus the list and ask it to flag anything dropped or no longer satisfied, then check each flag yourself.
