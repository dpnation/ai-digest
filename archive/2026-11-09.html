Anthropic explains how Claude agents maintain their own memory

Anthropic published research on agent memory: Claude saves notes as files, then uses downtime to check those notes against logs of past work and fix errors. The Deep View

Visa benchmark: shopping agents often buy when they shouldn't

Visa tested 22 models on realistic shopping tasks. The average pass rate was 57%, and more than half of failed runs still ended in a purchase. Models were also inconsistent when repeating scenarios they had passed.

Concrete evidence for keeping a human approval step before any agent takes an irreversible action, in marketing workflows and in Camunda's human-in-the-loop messaging.
