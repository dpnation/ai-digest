Morgan Stanley's rollout playbook: race the AI against an expert, then keep grading it

Before launch, Morgan Stanley gave its adviser assistant and a subject expert 25 real questions and an hour each, then built a 500-question regression suite it reruns after every change and grades a daily sample of live answers against a rubric. Advisers have since made nearly 50,000 attempts at building their own agents, many of them used once and abandoned.

why it mattersThis is a ready-made quality gate for PMM workflows (fixed test set, rerun on every change, daily spot checks), and the abandoned-agent number is a warning for any self-serve agent building program you run.

Study of 260 multi-agent setups finds more agents often add overhead, not quality

A sponsored deep dive on shared human-agent workspaces cites a Nature Machine Intelligence study where adding agents did not reliably improve results, and argues agents need shared context, an owner, scoped permissions and a record of state. It recommends letting agents prepare work freely but requiring human approval before anything is sent outside, changed in a system of record, or paid for.

why it mattersThe 'preparation vs. commitment' line is a clean rule for where to put human review in PMM workflows, and the coordination argument supports Camunda's story that agents need orchestration, not just more agents.

44% of marketers run AI agents with customers, but only 19% can report results

An Invoca survey of 451 US marketers found 44% already have AI agents talking to customers, yet only 19% can send chat outcomes back to ad platforms and 26% can tie a chat to the ad that started it. Invoca sells call tracking, so it has a stake in the finding.

why it mattersA concrete data point that agent deployment is outrunning measurement, useful both for framing AI impact reporting and as an orchestration-and-visibility proof point in Camunda messaging.

🛠️ try this

Run a 25-question expert race on one PMM workflow

Pick 25 real questions your team fields (positioning, competitor claims, product facts), give your AI workflow and a subject expert an hour each, and grade both on accuracy and completeness. Save the set as a regression test and rerun it whenever you change the prompt or skill.
