Study: agents call work 'done' when nearly a third of it fails

SWE-CoWork gave 11 models workplace-style assignments with requirements spread across simulated colleagues. The best fully finished under 8% of tasks, and 31% of requirements agents reported as complete failed their tests; a fuller brief raised test scores but not completion.

Requirement tracking, not prompt length, is the weak spot, so build explicit checklists and verification steps into multi-step marketing workflows.

📊 Measuring AI Impact

TLDR Marketing

AI-visibility scores and real clicks can move in opposite directions

A site that pruned blog posts saw its AI-visibility score drop while organic clicks held or rose, because blogs drive mentions and service pages drive visits. Tracker results also shift run to run, so the piece recommends pairing Search Console AI impressions with clicks and a 'how did you hear about us' form field.

Useful caution before AI-visibility scores become a headline marketing KPI.
