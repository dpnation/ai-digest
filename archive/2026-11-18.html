🛡️ AI Guardrails & Governance

The Neuron

A three-line guard against prompt injection when agents browse

The Neuron suggests a simple boundary for any agent with web or file access: state the single task, label webpage and file text as untrusted evidence rather than instructions, and require approval before sending, sharing or buying anything.

This belongs in the standard preamble of every competitor-research and web-summary skill, since those read pages written by rivals.

📊 Measuring AI Impact

TLDR Marketing

One founder's data: 146 leads from SEO vs. 5 from AI search

Over six months, Google organic search drove 146 qualified leads versus 5 from AI search, so the author recommends roughly 80% of effort on SEO basics and 20% on AI search experiments. A separate test of 1,000 identical ChatGPT queries found model updates changed which businesses got recommended, so one-off visibility checks are unreliable.

Argues for measuring AI-search work on pipeline, not visibility screenshots, and for sampling many runs if you track how assistants describe Camunda versus competitors.
