🛠️ try this

Make the agent list approved assets before it drafts

Attach your approved messaging house, boilerplate and proof points to a drafting skill, and require it to first list which approved claims and blocks it will reuse and flag anything they can't cover, before writing a word. Review that list, then let it draft; compare the result against a run without the step.
